🏎️⚡ F1's 2026 rules nearly TRIPLE the electric power (120kW → 350kW) and split power ~50/50 with the engine. So I asked: if energy management now wins races, what would the data look like?

I built a dashboard on a full 24-race season of lap-level ERS telemetry:
• Energy harvested vs deployed by team
• Regen-efficiency leaderboard (who recovers the most braking energy)
• Battery state-of-charge, lap by lap
• Which circuits are the most energy-hungry (hi Baku 👋)

Built the data model in Python (star schema, 28,920 lap-level rows) and designed it for Tableau & Power BI.
